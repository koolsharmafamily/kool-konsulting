# Kool Konsulting / The Kool Fold
Prepared 6 October 2026.

## One idea

Beautiful customer experiences connect to intelligent operations. The identity is a left-facing K followed by a right-facing K: **ꓘK**. The straight spines meet at the centre and the angled arms open outwards. This direction incorporates the owner's explicit correction. Never reverse their order.

The priority audiences are **startups, luxury brands and smart SMEs**. Hospitality demonstrates a potential application; it is not the main positioning.

## Logo

The full custom wordmark reads “kool konsulting”. Letters are editable vector paths; no raster text or installed font is required. Horizontal, stacked, ink monochrome, reversed porcelain and violet versions live in `public/brand`. The symbol is deliberately broad, so use the square favicon artwork for constrained app icons.

Clear space: at least half the symbol's cap height. Website horizontal logo: 163–242px in the tested header. Use a full-name accessible label. The small icon family includes 16, 24 and 32px PNG previews plus 192/512px app icons, a maskable icon and 180px Apple icon. Do not squeeze the symbol, add outlines, switch the K order or use the expressive sculpture instead of the functional logo.

No trademark clearance is claimed.

Core pairs: ink on porcelain, white on violet, porcelain on ink. Secondary text uses #565860 or a contrast-checked equivalent. Whitespace and thin rules create structure; dimensional lighting is reserved for the fold.

## Typography, spacing and icons

Self-hosted Manrope variable is the website family. IBM Plex Mono annotates short workflows. Installed font licences are SIL OFL 1.1; copies are in `public/brand/licenses`. The wordmark is separate geometry.

Desktop hero is approximately 80–96px, mobile 49px. Body copy is generally 15–18px; interactive form controls use 14–16px. Small captions belong to nonessential annotations, with semantic explanatory copy outside each composition.

Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 120. Main desktop container max 1328px; gutters reduce to 20px on phones. Corners are normally 2–4px. Lucide icons: 1.5–2px strokes, 16–24px; icon-only controls need accessible labels.

## Voice

Precise, intelligent, warm, quietly confident. “Kool” can carry light wit, while service and commercial language stays clear. Use Indian English. No invented results, customer logos, team size, urgency or performance guarantees.

Core promise: “Beautiful experiences. Intelligent operations.”
CTA: “Book a call.” Without a real scheduler the destination says “Request a call” and describes the actual outcome.

## Motion and fallback

The fold reveals three layers: experience, connection, outcome. Interaction responds immediately, with 160–280ms control transitions and an approximately 700ms user-triggered unfold. No looping motion, scroll hijacking, artificial delays or WebGL requirement. The hero uses an original generated material composition, split into CSS perspective layers for unfolding; it is not a live WebGL model. Essential labels and controls are HTML. Reduced motion keeps the complete composition and immediate state changes.
