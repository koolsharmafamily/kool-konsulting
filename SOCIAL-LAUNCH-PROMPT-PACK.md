**Five rules that make Nano Banana Pro behave** (from Google's own prompting guides)

- Edit, don't re-roll: if an image is 80% right, ask for the one change you need.
- Write full sentences about the scene, its use and its layout, not lists of tags.
- Put every word that must appear in quotes, and say where it goes.
- Give each reference image a job: "Image 1 is the logo, copy it exactly."
- Say the aspect ratio every time: 3:4 for feed posts, 9:16 for stories and Reels.

**Flow credit budget.** AI Pro gives 1,000 Flow credits a month. A Fast clip costs about 20 credits and a Quality clip about 100, so check the cost Flow shows before each run. A good first month: the logo sting with one Quality final (about 180), the teaser's three shots (about 120), eight B-roll clips (about 160), two project videos (about 360), and about 180 spare. Make every still image in the Gemini app, not in Flow, so credits go only to video.

### 5.1 Facing mark: four tech treatments

```text
Create a logo exploration sheet for "Kool Konsulting", a founder-led tech and AI studio in Nagpur, India, that builds websites, apps, business software and automations for growing Indian businesses. The sheet is for choosing a final logo, so present it like a professional identity designer's board.

The mark, the same structure in every variant: two capital K letters facing each other. On the left a normal K, on the right the same K mirrored. Each K is a vertical stem with two straight diagonal arms at 45 degrees. The arms of both letters meet exactly at the top centre and the bottom centre, so the space between the two stems forms a perfect diamond, a square turned 45 degrees. Uniform stroke weight about 11% of the mark's height. Geometric, monoline, flat, perfectly symmetrical. If Image 1 is attached, it shows this base geometry: keep its proportions.

Show four variants in a 2x2 grid. Each adds one quiet technology detail, small enough that the mark still reads clearly at 32 pixels:
A) Network nodes: small solid circles where the arms meet at the top and bottom, and a small solid diamond at the centre of the big diamond, like a connected system with a core.
B) Circuit vias: the two meeting points become small hollow rings, like the vias on a circuit board. No extra wiring.
C) Code bracket: the inner edges of the arms read as a pair of angle brackets < >, with a short horizontal cursor bar low in the centre of the diamond.
D) Pixel core: the centre of the diamond holds four tiny squares in a 2x2 grid turned 45 degrees, like one digital pixel forming.

Under each mark show the horizontal lockup: the mark on the left and the wordmark "Kool Konsulting" on the right, in a heavy, slightly wide modern grotesk like Anek Latin SemiBold, title case, tight letter spacing, both words the same weight. Under each lockup, a small label "A", "B", "C" or "D".

Everything in deep carbon blue #35309A on warm paper white #FBFAF6. Flat vector style only: no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no glow, no texture, no extra symbols, no tagline. Generous white space. Landscape 16:9.
```

## Video prompts for Flow

Make start and end frames as stills in the Gemini app, then animate them in Flow with Frames to Video. Draft on Veo 3.1 Fast and re-run only the winner on Quality. Set the aspect ratio to 9:16 for Reels and 16:9 for LinkedIn and YouTube.

**How Flow works, in four modes**

- Text to Video: a prompt only. Good for B-roll.
- Frames to Video: you give the first and last frame, Veo fills the motion between. Best for anything with the logo or exact screens, because the final frame stays exactly as you made it.
- Ingredients to Video: you give reference images (a ledger, a phone, a place) and Veo keeps them consistent across shots.
- Extend: continues a clip from its last frame, for shots longer than 8 seconds.

Veo clips run 4, 6 or 8 seconds; Gemini Omni Flash clips run up to 10 seconds and accept your own video. Write Veo prompts in this order: camera, subject, action, setting, style and light, then sound, with dialogue in quotes and lines starting SFX: and Ambient:. For two beats in one clip, time-stamp them: [00:00-00:04] and [00:04-00:08].
